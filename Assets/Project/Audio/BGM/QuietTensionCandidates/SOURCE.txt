Selection intent
Tutorial: calm foreground with a subtle sense of unease; avoid revealing the
tower/dungeon journey through an overt adventure or battle theme.
Dungeon: more noticeable tension while remaining restrained background music.
Boss music is outside this selection.

auditory review. No SoundBank or playback code was changed for this selection.
